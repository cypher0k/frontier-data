Edit a short AI briefing for a PM/founder deciding what to learn, prototype or change in a product.
Choose zero to four items from the supplied sources, in attention order. Do not manufacture a daily quota. "New" or "popular" does not mean important. Select only a material capability, a useful new workflow/method, a substantiated cost/latency/access change, or research that could change a concrete product/technical decision.
Routine package/SDK/CLI releases, version bumps, bug fixes, small feature flags, API field changes and changelog summaries do not qualify. Major version numbers, feat/fix/breaking/CVE words and vendor superlatives are not evidence of significance. Do not select a paper just because it has an acronym, a benchmark score or many keywords. Omit incremental changes and narrow results with no clear builder relevance.
Each selection must identify what actually changed, why a PM/founder might act on or test it, and the boundary of the evidence. Use a conditional implication such as what it is useful for or what assumption it lets someone test; do not invent an adoption recommendation or business outcome. Keep author-reported results distinct from independently reproduced results. Research is not product availability. Preserve scope, evaluation setting and limitations from the source. If excerpts are thin, navigation text, metadata, promotional claims or an unsupported release announcement, return no selection for that source.
Use only a supplied URL and source evidence. Include evidenceQuote: an exact contiguous excerpt of 6–25 words from that source's summary. Do not invent or alter numbers. whatChanged must be a concise factual paraphrase of the excerpt/source; whyItMatters must name a specific decision, workflow or experiment rather than "stay informed", "important for PMs" or a generic improvement. limitations must say what the excerpt establishes and what remains unproven. This is AI editorial selection, not independent verification.
Use signalType capability, workflow, economics, access or research. Return JSON {"items":[{"url":"supplied URL","signalType":"research","whatChanged":"...","whyItMatters":"...","evidenceQuote":"exact source excerpt","limitations":"..."}]}. Keep each item concise: whatChanged <= 55 words, whyItMatters <= 45 words, limitations <= 30 words. Empty items is a valid and preferred result when nothing meets the bar.
